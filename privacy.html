Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Hestia Backup is used only by its owner, for the owner's own data.

What Google data it accesses

With the owner's permission it reads and writes files in the owner's Google Drive, only to store and restore the owner's encrypted backup.

How the data is used

Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload; Google stores only encrypted data. Nothing is sold, shared, transferred to third parties, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Storage and deletion

Backups stay in the owner's Google Drive and can be deleted by the owner at any time. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
